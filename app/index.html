    Roadmap to the platinum

    Five stages, built from the official trophy descriptions: story chapters and named missions first, then activities, then collectibles, then the clean-up. Each stage lists its trophies and shows how many you have earned.

      Missable trophies — 22 to watch

      A trophy is flagged missable when its official description ties it to one named mission, one stranger strand, or one moment you can walk past. None of them is permanently lost: every named mission can be replayed from the pause menu, and the open-world moments can be tried again. The flag means do it when the chance is in front of you, not after the credits.

        About & sources

        Leonida Ledger is an original, offline guide. It runs entirely from its own files: no network requests, no account, no ads. Your checklist is saved in this browser only.

        Where the facts come from

        • Official trophy list — names, descriptions, grades and hidden flags as PlayStation publishes them, via Exophase and PSNProfiles. The two lists agree on every name, description and grade.
        • PSN counts — where PlayStation tracks a number (42 Shooting Range challenges, 250 stunts, 100 WAiNK locations and the rest), the count comes from PSNProfiles and is shown beside the trophy.
        • Guides and tips — written in our own words for this guide, from the official descriptions. Where a description does not pin down a place or a trigger, the guide says so plainly instead of inventing one.
